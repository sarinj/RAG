AstraRail Operations Knowledge Base

1. Company Overview

AstraRail is a fictional passenger rail company founded in 2012. It operates high-speed and regional train services across the fictional country of Norland.

As of 2026, AstraRail operates 84 passenger trains and serves 37 cities. Its busiest route connects Northport and Velden, carrying approximately 22,000 passengers per weekday.

AstraRail has three ticket classes:
- Standard
- Comfort
- Executive

Executive Class includes lounge access, meals, and flexible ticket changes.

2. Ticket Changes and Refunds

Standard tickets may be changed up to 24 hours before departure for a fee of 15 Norland Dollars.

Comfort tickets may be changed up to 6 hours before departure without a change fee, although passengers must pay any fare difference.

Executive tickets may be changed at any time before departure without a change fee.

Standard tickets are refundable only if cancelled at least 48 hours before departure. A 20% cancellation fee applies.

Comfort tickets are refundable until 12 hours before departure with a 10% cancellation fee.

Executive tickets are fully refundable until departure.

3. Baggage Policy

Each Standard passenger may bring one large suitcase weighing up to 20 kilograms and one small carry-on bag.

Comfort passengers may bring up to 30 kilograms of checked baggage.

Executive passengers may bring up to 40 kilograms of checked baggage.

Bicycles are allowed only on regional trains and require a bicycle reservation.

Electric scooters with batteries larger than 500 watt-hours are not permitted onboard.

4. Delay Compensation

Passengers may request compensation when a train arrives at its destination at least 60 minutes late.

For delays between 60 and 119 minutes, passengers receive 25% of the ticket price.

For delays of 120 minutes or more, passengers receive 50% of the ticket price.

Compensation is not provided when the delay was caused by severe weather, police activity, or emergency track closures.

Passengers must submit compensation requests within 30 days of the journey.

5. Project Horizon

Project Horizon is AstraRail's program to modernize its reservation platform.

The project began in January 2026 and is led by Elena Novak.

Its main goals are:
- Reduce booking-page response time.
- Support real-time seat availability.
- Improve mobile booking reliability.
- Introduce dynamic pricing.

The legacy reservation system refreshes seat availability every 60 seconds.

Project Horizon is designed to update seat availability within 2 seconds.

The first production rollout is planned for December 2026.

6. Train Maintenance

High-speed trains receive a safety inspection every 15,000 kilometers.

A more extensive maintenance service is performed every 120,000 kilometers.

Wheel assemblies are replaced based on measured wear rather than a fixed calendar schedule.

Brake systems have both scheduled inspections and continuous sensor monitoring.

If a critical brake sensor reports a fault, the train must be removed from passenger service until an engineer clears it.

7. Customer Support

Customer support is available from 06:00 to 23:00 every day.

Emergency assistance for passengers currently traveling is available 24 hours a day.

Lost-property requests are kept open for 60 days.

If an item is found, AstraRail stores it for 90 days before it may be donated or disposed of.
